### code
- `administration/step.js`: `factionOf`, `questionScope`, `durationOf`, `assemblyOf`, `countryOf`, `needOf`, `regionOf`. `queue.js`: assemblies are `region:<id>` or `empire:<country>` (`assemblyCoordination`, `agendaOf`, `parseAssembly`); `openDebates` opens a debate with `{since, duration, assembly, holder, scope}` by the scope of the Question's faction. `module.js`: the readout rows carry `scope`, `petition` and `handling`; the Country's assembly appears when a multi-Region or country-wide Question exists.

- `polity/step.js`: the expiry modifier goes to the faction through `factionOf`, multiplied by `petition.expiryMult` on a petitioning cell, and is skipped when `conceded` reached `petition.comparable`; `petitionRefusal` (a petitioning cell's Question that nobody discusses for `petition.grace` turns takes a large slow Fairness modifier once); the petition clock is dropped with the Question.
