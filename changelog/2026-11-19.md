## First full test run of slice 3b: fixes, and the designer's debate rules recorded — 2026-11-20

**Test run read** (`test_errors.md` of 2026-11-20, 194 tests: 186 passed, 8 failed; the pasted list of 49 failures with `missing tuning key opinion.alignGain` / `zone.openFloor` was an older run made before `npm run build:data`: it is superseded). All of the new tests of slice 3b and of free colonization / the slate consumer passed (`worldmap_migration`, `slate_colonize`, `administration`).

### The 8 failures and their causes
- **5 × `Maximum call stack size exceeded`** (`editor` round trip, `internal_conflict` × 2, `war` run, and the war tests' turns): `polity/transfer.js` `splitObligations` made a NEW debt record for every transfer of every obligation naming the source cell, and `obs.push(...added)` overflowed the argument stack. Repeated small transfers (gradual migration every turn, 118 provinces) multiplied the records geometrically. **Fixed**: a share now joins an existing record with the same terms (same debtor, creditor and other fields; that record must not name the source) instead of becoming a new one, so the records stay bounded by the pairs of cells; the list is appended in a loop. Mobility and Demotion use the same function and keep their semantics (the first split still creates `<id>~<target>`).
- **`pop.growth = 0 switches it off exactly`** (41680.00000000001 vs 41680) and **two war tests** (free labour 22.99425 vs 23; a cell's population above its earlier value): migration was moving people in the demo from turn 1, which these tests do not expect. **Fixed in the tests**: they switch migration off through the tuning (`mig.rate` 0), as they already switch growth off; a new conservation test (`population.test.js`) runs the demo for 20 turns with growth off and migration on and asserts that no person is created or lost and that the debt records stay under 5000.
- **`worldmap_reorg`: ops chain** (`targets()` returned `['r12', 'r12']`): a split followed by a merge of the two parts reaches the same Region twice. **Fixed** in `reorg.js` (each target once). This was a bug of the 2026-11-17 code: the test file had not been run before (the designer's `autotest.bat` carries an older, shorter file list than `npm test`: it lacks `administration`, `worldmap_reorg`, `worldmap_migration`, `slate_colonize`, `debate`; it needs the new names added).
- Not seen: the `ai_diplomacy` failure of the older list (`campaign: a new CB bypasses the margin`) is gone in the new run.

### Designer's debate rules (2026-11-20), recorded in `administration__kb.md` (Question Active Slate rev 3, Assembly Queue rev 2, Administration actions rev 2, Rules rev 3)
- The freeze lasts only while the Question is discussed (the length of its debate, plus the turns the Government prolongs it).
- After the debate: resolved in a way the involved actors accept → the Question stops being pressing, or fades if it matters less because of how it was resolved; not solved acceptably (an expired debate) → it becomes a pressing issue again, the estates that asked and were not satisfied take a minor decaying grievance modifier, then the usual mechanics continue.
- New Government action `extendDebates`: a commitment holding the same share of Control and Coordination, growing quadratically with every turn of prolongation beyond the natural timespan.
