### spec (`spec/world_map__spec.md`, Population dynamics rewritten; Sync: Population dynamics 1 / 1 provisional)
- One move primitive, `moveCohort`: caps by population and room, mirrors province stock and presence, hands the Estate cell's share to another Region through `transferCohort` (the destination cell is created when missing), optional confiscation to the Government, then Wasteland → Claimed/Frontier → Settled by absolute population.
- Gradual migration is derived at step 8 (after growth, before queued moves and Reorganizations). Province-level satisfaction does not exist (cells are per Region), so the pull reads the Region's population-weighted `growthFactor`; `mig.frontierPull` is new so that unsettled provinces of the same Region can fill at all. `MovementAutonomy` multiplies the flow; a `LaborMigrationBan` blocks a pair.
- Settlement route 2 is the cell action `settle`; route 3b is `directResettlement`; Expulsion is `expel`. All three queue into `world.pendingMoves` and run at step 8 (re-validated, a stale order is dropped whole).
