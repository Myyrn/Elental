**What was built (`src/modules/administration/`)**
- New `step.js` — pipeline **step 6**, registered in `module.js` (`steps: {6}`): for each Country it writes the Government's attention for **next turn** (`gov.attention = [{questionKey, coordinationCommitted}]`) and `gov.standing` (routine Questions handled by standing administration).
  - Budget = new derive `administration.coordinationSupply(country)` = `gov.coordinationSupply ?? produceCCO(country).coordination` (the override has the same role as `controlSupply`; degraded output from unmet upkeep is already in `produceCCO`; Emergency Powers' cost will be subtracted here later).
  - **Routine tier has no slate commitment** (kb, settled 2026-11-02): each handled routine Question costs `admin.routineOverhead`; when Coordination runs short the lowest-priority routine Questions drop out of `standing` (never removed).

  - Then existential and structural Questions in order (tier, then priority, then key): a Question is taken when the remaining Coordination covers its fixed threshold `slate.existential` / `slate.structural`; one that does not fit is skipped and lower ones that still fit are taken (existential crowds out, never removes).
  - `questionPriority = gap × Weight(cell) × (cell holds the Petition Right ? queue.petitionMult : 1)` — the instantiation of polity's `queue_priority`.
- New tuning key `queue.petitionMult` 2 in `scenarios/alpha/tuning.defaults.json` (pushed whole: `git diff` should show only that key). `slate.routine` stays in the file but is not read (the old spec sketch used it; the kb says routine Questions have no threshold).

**Designer decisions applied (all four, plus "I confirm your proposals on implementation")**
1. *An aligned cell also gains*, a minor-to-moderate modifier growing with how well the Government's behaviour matches the cell's ideal image: per axis `match = 1 − |pref − observed|` (+1 at the ideal, −1 at opposite poles), term = `|observed| × (match > 0 ? opinion.alignGain × match : match)`; new key `opinion.alignGain` 0.5.
2. *A standing law may fade* — nothing to build; recorded as settled for alpha.
3. *Direction matters*: a tax rise or a bigger requisition is Extraction, a tax cut or a smaller/lifted requisition is Investment. Built as an optional action-type hook `axisShift(ctx, a)` evaluated by the engine before `apply` and snapshotted on the Decision Log entry as `loads`; `setTaxLaw` (value-weighted change of the take over the covered cells of the acting Country, rate-sum as fallback) and `placeRequisition` (new amount against the existing entry for that good) implement it. Entries without `loads` fall back to the type's static `axisLoads`.
4. *Portrait reinforcement trigger = Government actions/behaviour*: every executed Government act (public or covert) pushes its Country's Portrait one step (`portrait.reinforce`, 0.05) toward the pole it acts on, in step 5 after the stack decay.
